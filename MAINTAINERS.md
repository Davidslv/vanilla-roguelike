## Becoming a maintainer

There is no fixed process yet. A contributor who has landed several good pull requests and reviewed others' work may be invited to help maintain an area (for example, maze algorithms or the test harness). Maintainers are listed here and in [`.github/CODEOWNERS`](.github/CODEOWNERS).
