Damage Calculation:
===================

┌──────────────────────────────────┐
│  Attacker Stats                  │
│  ├─> Attack Power: 10            │
│  └─> Accuracy: 0.8 (80%)         │
└──────────────────────────────────┘
              │
              ▼
┌──────────────────────────────────┐
│  Roll for Hit                    │
│  rand < accuracy?                │
│  └─> 0.65 < 0.8 → HIT!           │
└──────────────────────────────────┘
              │
              ▼
┌──────────────────────────────────┐
│  Defender Stats                  │
│  └─> Defense: 5                  │
└──────────────────────────────────┘
              │
              ▼
┌──────────────────────────────────┐
│  Calculate Damage                │
│  damage = attack - defense       │
│  damage = 10 - 5                 │
│  damage = 5                      │
└──────────────────────────────────┘
              │
              ▼
┌──────────────────────────────────┐
│  Minimum Damage Check            │
│  damage = max(damage, 1)         │
│  └─> Ensures at least 1 damage   │
└──────────────────────────────────┘
              │
              ▼
┌──────────────────────────────────┐
│  Apply to Health                 │
│  current_health -= damage        │
│  50 - 5 = 45                     │
└──────────────────────────────────┘

Event Emission:
===============

Combat Events:
──────────────

:combat_damage
  ├─> target_id
  ├─> damage
  └─> source_id

:combat_death
  ├─> entity_id
  └─> killer_id


Subscribers can react:
──────────────────────

LootSystem
  └─> Subscribes to :combat_death
      └─> Generates loot when monster dies

StatisticsSystem
  └─> Subscribes to :combat_damage
      └─> Tracks damage dealt

MessageSystem
  └─> Subscribes to both events
      └─> Displays combat messages


Example Combat Scenario:
=========================

Initial State:
──────────────

Player                    Monster
├─ Position: [5, 3]       ├─ Position: [5, 4]
├─ Health: 100/100        ├─ Health: 50/50
├─ Combat:                ├─ Combat:
│  ├─ Attack: 15          │  ├─ Attack: 10
│  ├─ Defense: 8          │  ├─ Defense: 5
│  └─ Accuracy: 0.9       │  └─ Accuracy: 0.7
└─                        └─


Player Attacks:
───────────────

1. Roll: 0.85 < 0.9 → HIT!
2. Damage: 15 - 5 = 10
3. Apply: 50 - 10 = 40 HP

Monster Health: 40/50


Monster Counter-Attacks:
─────────────────────────

1. Roll: 0.75 < 0.7 → MISS!
2. No damage

Player Health: 100/100 (unchanged)


Second Player Attack:
──────────────────────

1. Roll: 0.65 < 0.9 → HIT!
2. Damage: 15 - 5 = 10
3. Apply: 40 - 10 = 30 HP

Monster Health: 30/50


Eventually:
───────────

Player Attack:
1. Roll: 0.45 < 0.9 → HIT!
2. Damage: 15 - 5 = 10
3. Apply: 10 - 10 = 0 HP

Monster Health: 0/50 → DEAD!

Events:
  ├─> :combat_death emitted
  └─> Monster removed from world


Minimum Damage Rule:
=====================

Why?
────
Ensures combat always progresses.
Even heavily armored enemies take damage.

Example:
────────

Weak Attack vs Strong Defense:
  attack: 3
  defense: 10
  damage: 3 - 10 = -7

  Apply minimum: max(-7, 1) = 1

  Result: 1 damage (not 0!)


Components Make Combat Flexible:
=================================

Want different combat types?
Just modify components!

Strong Attacker:
  CombatComponent(attack: 20, defense: 5)

Tank (High Defense):
  CombatComponent(attack: 8, defense: 15)

Glass Cannon:
  CombatComponent(attack: 25, defense: 2)
  HealthComponent(max_health: 30)

Boss:
  CombatComponent(attack: 30, defense: 20)
  HealthComponent(max_health: 500)
